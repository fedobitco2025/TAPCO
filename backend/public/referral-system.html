Referral Guide

Referral growth in TAPCO is controlled, reviewed, and policy-bound

Referral systems only stay valuable when they reward real user acquisition instead of synthetic loops. TAPCO therefore treats referral credit as a governed outcome, not an automatic promise attached to every shared link.

How a referral is evaluated

A referral begins when a user arrives through a valid entry route, but that entry alone does not finalize credit. TAPCO may evaluate uniqueness, account legitimacy, session quality, suspicious repetition patterns, and conflicts with prior linked identities. This prevents self-referral farms, recycled traffic, and coordinated abuse clusters.

A strong referral system must protect both the invited player and the referring player. If invalid traffic is over-credited, honest participants lose trust. If the system rejects too aggressively without explanation, confusion grows. The practical solution is public clarity plus careful background verification.

Uniqueness

Repeated identities and duplicate clusters may fail validation.

Limits

Referral-related boundaries may evolve with risk and economy conditions.

Rules and practical limits

  • Referral status depends on validated user activity, not raw clicks alone.
  • Identity conflicts, suspicious device overlap, or abuse indicators can block referral credit.
  • Platform-side limits may be adjusted to protect fairness and system sustainability.
  • Users who believe a valid referral was missed should contact support with timing and account details.

Referral notice: TAPCO GAME referrals are engagement mechanics in an entertainment product, not guaranteed income promises.

Concrete referral credit path

  • User enters through a valid referral route.
  • Identity and session checks verify that the referral is legitimate.
  • Suspicious duplication patterns can pause settlement for review.
  • Valid referrals are reflected after policy checks are completed.
