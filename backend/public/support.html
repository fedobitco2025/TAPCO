Support & Help

Official support guidance for TAPCO users

This page is adapted from the in-game support center and rewritten for the public website. It explains how to prepare a useful report, which issues support can help with, and what security precautions every user should follow.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm your internet connection is stable.
  • Make sure your Telegram session or app state is current.
  • Prepare your player or account context before reporting.

Best report format

Describe what happened, when it happened, and what you expected instead. Add screenshots when possible. Clear reports reduce back-and-forth and speed up investigation.

Support scope

Support can help with gameplay consistency, account access questions, referral visibility concerns, mission or progress confusion, and general product understanding.

Security warning

Never share private keys, seed phrases, one-time codes, or any wallet secret with anyone claiming to be support. TAPCO support will not ask for those secrets.
