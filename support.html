Common Checks

  • Confirm you are using the official TAPCO Telegram entry point.
  • Refresh Telegram session if gameplay data appears stale.
  • Verify referral link format and avoid altered query strings.
  • Do not use VPN/proxy tools that may trigger identity mismatch controls.

Before You Report

  • Include timestamp, your Telegram username, and a short issue summary.
  • Attach screenshots where possible.
  • Send reports to support@tapcogame.io for official review.
