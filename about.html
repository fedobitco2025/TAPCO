About TAPCO

TAPCO is a Telegram-native game experience built around transparent progression, clear rules, and fair play expectations. Our goal is to create a sustainable product where users understand how points, rewards, and referral systems work without hidden mechanics.

من نحن في TAPCO

TAPCO تجربة لعب مصممة لتيليغرام، وتقوم على التدرج الواضح والقواعد الشفافة ومبدأ اللعب النظيف. هدفنا بناء منتج مستدام يفهم فيه اللاعب آلية النقاط والمكافآت والإحالات بدون أي تفاصيل غامضة.

TAPCO Hakkinda

TAPCO, seffaf ilerleme, net kurallar ve adil oyun prensibine dayali Telegram odakli bir oyun deneyimidir. Amacimiz; puanlarin, odullerin ve referans sisteminin gizli mekanikler olmadan anlasildigi surdurulebilir bir urun kurmaktir.

Vision and Product Principles

  • Gameplay remains inside Telegram for consistency and account security controls.
  • This website acts as an official documentation and support hub.
  • No guaranteed profit claims, no deceptive earning language, and no misleading growth promises.
  • Anti-abuse posture: bot activity, fraud attempts, and exploit behavior are subject to action.

Operational Transparency

TAPCO maintains clear public pages for privacy, terms, ad practices, and support channels. Players can contact support through official channels and report issues regarding access, referrals, account consistency, or policy questions.
