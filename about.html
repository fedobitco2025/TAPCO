About TAPCO

How TAPCO works in practice and what users can verify publicly

TAPCO GAME runs as a Telegram mini app with server-authoritative gameplay accounting. This means key progression values are validated on the backend before they are committed. This website exists so players can read concrete rules, support steps, and policy boundaries in one place.

Our Product Commitments

  • Tap batches are processed with hard limits and validation before score is credited.
  • Core upgrade purchases use server-side price calculation and score deduction.
  • Financially sensitive flows require fresh Telegram identity validation.
  • Policy, support, and update pages are public so users can verify expectations.
