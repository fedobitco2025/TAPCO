Referral System Overview

TAPCO referral logic rewards authentic user growth. The system is designed to block fake loops, self-referrals, and synthetic traffic patterns that undermine fairness.

Eligibility Conditions

  • The referred account must be unique and compliant with platform rules.
  • Events suspected as automated or duplicate can be excluded.
  • Final referral status is determined by server validation and anti-fraud checks.

Abuse Prevention

TAPCO may reject, postpone, or revoke referral credit when abuse indicators appear, including collusive account clusters, device fingerprint overlaps, or repeated identity conflicts. These controls protect honest users and platform stability.
