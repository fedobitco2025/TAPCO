Advertising Policy

TAPCO applies strict separation between website content advertising and gameplay reward mechanics.

سياسة الإعلانات

تطبق TAPCO فصلا صارما بين إعلانات المحتوى داخل الموقع وبين آليات مكافآت اللعب.

Reklam Politikasi

TAPCO, site icerik reklamlari ile oyun odul mekaniklerini kesin bicimde ayirir.

Policy Principles

  • No instructions encouraging users to click content ads for rewards.
  • No forced ad-click flows for website ad placements.
  • Third-party ad providers remain responsible for delivered creative content.
  • TAPCO reserves the right to block harmful ad categories where controls are available.

User Choice and Reporting

Users can report inappropriate ad experiences via support@tapcogame.io. Ad preference controls may be available through provider dashboards such as Google ad settings.
