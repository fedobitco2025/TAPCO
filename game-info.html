How TAPCO Works

TAPCO follows a tap-to-earn style loop inside Telegram: players interact, progress through systems, and unlock milestones. The platform uses server-side validation and anti-abuse controls to reduce manipulation.

كيف تعمل TAPCO

TAPCO Nasil Calisir

TAPCO, Telegram icinde tap-to-earn dongusu kullanir: oyuncular etkilesime girer, sistemlerde ilerler ve kilometre taslarina ulasir. Platform, manipule girisimlerini azaltmak icin sunucu tarafi dogrulama ve anti-abuse kontrolleri uygular.

Core Systems

  • Progress points are calculated through controlled gameplay events.
  • Referral outcomes are bounded by eligibility and abuse checks.
